FlowSpace Support

Speech practice, your way.

FlowSpace helps you practice speaking with paragraphs built around your sounds, drills, free speaking, real-world missions, and a record of what you're learning. Everything stays on your phone.

Need help or have an idea? Email flowspace.support.help@gmail.com. I read every message.

Where is my practice history?

On your phone only. FlowSpace has no accounts and no servers. If you delete the app, your history is deleted with it. You can send yourself a copy first with Settings, then Your Data, then Export My Data.

How do I delete my data?

Open Settings in FlowSpace and tap Erase All My Data. You can also delete the app.

The microphone isn't working.

Open the iPhone Settings app, scroll to FlowSpace, and turn on Microphone. Then try recording again.

I'm not getting reminders.

Open the iPhone Settings app, tap Notifications, then FlowSpace, and allow notifications. In FlowSpace, make sure Daily Reminder is on in Settings and check the time you picked.

Is FlowSpace a treatment?

No. It is a practice tool, not medical advice or treatment. A speech-language pathologist can give you guidance made for you. See the links below to find one.
