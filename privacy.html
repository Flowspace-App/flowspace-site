FlowSpace Privacy Policy

Effective October 13, 2026

The short version

FlowSpace keeps everything on your phone. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking. Nothing you do in FlowSpace is sent to us or to anyone else.

What FlowSpace stores on your phone

Your practice sessions (the words and letters you marked, effort ratings, and notes), your goals, missions, monthly check-ins, streak days, and your settings. This stays on your device. If you delete the app, it is deleted too.

The microphone

FlowSpace uses the microphone only while you are recording a practice. The recording is used to play it back to you in the app. It is not uploaded or shared, and FlowSpace does not keep a library of your recordings. Your phone may clear its temporary files on its own schedule.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, your phone schedules it locally. No information is sent anywhere to do this. You can turn it off in FlowSpace or in iPhone Settings at any time.

Links to other sites

The Guide has links to organizations like the National Stuttering Association and the Stuttering Foundation. When you open one, you leave FlowSpace, and that site has its own privacy policy.

Your choices

You can erase everything FlowSpace has saved with Erase All My Data in Settings. You can send yourself a copy with Export My Data. Where it goes after that is up to you.

Children

FlowSpace does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Not medical advice

FlowSpace is a practice tool. It is not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. A speech-language pathologist can give you guidance made for you.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear here and in the app, with a new date.
